## 1. The Syntactic Tax of Autoregressive Chain-of-Thought

When a Small Language Model (SLM) solves a multi-step reasoning problem via standard Chain-of-Thought (CoT), every intermediate step is forced through a discrete vocabulary bottleneck. At decoding step $t$, the top-layer hidden state $h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ is projected against the vocabulary unembedding matrix $W_{\text{unembed}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times \lvert V \rvert}$ to sample a single discrete token, which is then re-embedded and appended to the key-value (KV) cache for step $t + 1$.<Sidenote id={1} title="Unembedding Bandwidth Bottleneck">For `Qwen2.5-1.5B` ${(d = 1536,}$ ${\lvert V \rvert = 151{,}936)},$ projecting $h_t^{(L)} \cdot W_{\text{unembed}}$ alone requires ${2 d \lvert V \rvert = 4.67 \times 10^8}$ FLOPs and streams ${\approx 445\text{ MB}}$ of FP16 unembedding weights per emitted token.</Sidenote>

Crucially, natural-language training corpora entangle **state-transition tokens**—numeric literals, algebraic operators, variable bindings, and intermediate quantities—with **syntactic fluency tokens** such as discourse markers (*"Let's think step by step"*), determiners (*"the"*, *"a"*), copulas (*"is"*, *"was"*), and prepositional scaffolding. In memory-bandwidth-bound SLM deployments, emitting a syntactic article like `"the"` incurs the exact same per-token KV-cache growth and $W_{\text{unembed}}$ projection cost as committing a pivotal arithmetic result like `"72"`.

$$
p(y_t \mid y_{\lt t}, x) = \operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{RMSNorm}\!\left(h_t^{(L)}\right) W_{\text{unembed}}}{\tau}\right), \quad h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d, \; W_{\text{unembed}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times \lvert V \rvert}
$$

To isolate how much of CoT's compute budget actually advances the underlying reasoning state versus maintaining grammatical well-formedness—trained end-to-end in PyTorch on `Qwen3-0.6B` via [`experiments/src/qwen3_ablation.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/qwen3_ablation.py), [`experiments/src/train_pipeline.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/train_pipeline.py), and [`experiments/scripts/run_qwen3_ablation.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/scripts/run_qwen3_ablation.py), exported via [`experiments/scripts/export_web_artifacts.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/scripts/export_web_artifacts.py), and configured in [`experiments/src/config.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/config.py)—we evaluate four controlled inference regimes across `Qwen3-0.6B` ${(0.6\text{B}),}$ `Qwen2.5-1.5B` ${(1.5\text{B}),}$ and `Qwen2.5-3B` ${(3.0\text{B})}$ on **GSM8K** and **MATH**:

1. **Verbose CoT (`verbose_cot`, Baseline)**: Unconstrained grammatical Chain-of-Thought generation.
2. **Telegraphic CoT (`telegraphic_cot`, Motivational Ablation A)**: Syntactically pruned traces ([`experiments/src/prune_syntax.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/prune_syntax.py)) serving strictly as an empirical control that strips grammatical glue while preserving state-transition tokens.
3. **Compute-Matched / Budget-Forced CoT (`compute_matched`, Motivational Ablation B)**: Standard verbose CoT truncated/forced to match the exact token and FLOP budget of Telegraphic CoT.
4. **Latent Loop + Ring-Buffer Tee (`latent_loop`, Proposed Architecture — Ablation C)**: $K$ recurrent state transitions $z_k \in \mathbb{R}^d$ executed directly in continuous latent space ([`experiments/src/latent_loop.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/latent_loop.py)), paired with a fixed-capacity detachable `RingBufferLatentTee` ($M \le K$) and stop-gradient `SidecarProbe` ($g_{\phi}(\operatorname{sg}[z_{1:K}])$).

On MATH, **$K=20$ achieves 20.0% accuracy** at **4.5 tokens total** (saving **62.4% FLOPs**).

On `Qwen2.5-1.5B`, $K = 8$ achieves $73.9\%$ accuracy, and $K = 16$ reaches $75.2\%$ ($86.7\%$ on `3.0B`).

## 3. Ablation A: Syntactic Pruning as a Motivational Control

Why does Telegraphic CoT preserve reasoning fidelity while cutting token volume by two-thirds? A chain of thought can be decomposed into a sequence of discrete variable bindings $\mathcal{S} = \{s_1, s_2, \dots, s_M\}$ interleaved with syntactic tokens $\mathcal{G}$ that satisfy surface English grammar. Importantly, **Telegraphic CoT is not our proposed final architecture**—it serves as an empirical control isolating the syntactic tax before moving reasoning into $\mathbb{R}^d$. In our syntactic pruning utility ([`experiments/src/prune_syntax.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/prune_syntax.py)), each token is classified into one of four functional strata:<Sidenote id={3} title="Stratified Pruning Thresholds">Tokens are pruned monotonically by grammatical stratum: discourse scaffolding first ${(\tau \in [0.04, 0.36]),}$ determiners and auxiliaries second ${(\tau \in [0.37, 0.68]),}$ and prepositions or conjunctions third ${(\tau \in [0.69, 0.99]),}$ while state-transition tokens carry ${\tau = 1.50}$ and are never pruned.</Sidenote>

1. **Discourse Markers ($\tau \in [0.04, 0.36]$)**: Conversational framing (*"Let's"*, *"think"*, *"step"*, *"First"*, *"Therefore"*, *"calculate"*).
2. **Determiners &amp; Auxiliaries ($\tau \in [0.37, 0.68]$)**: Articles, pronouns, and copulas (*"the"*, *"a"*, *"is"*, *"was"*, *"she"*, *"that"*).
3. **Prepositions &amp; Conjunctions ($\tau \in [0.69, 0.99]$)**: Relational connectors (*"of"*, *"in"*, *"to"*, *"for"*, *"because"*, *"and"*).
4. **State Transitions (Preserved)**: Numeric values, algebraic operators (`=`, `+`, `-`, `/`, `^`), and bound state identifiers (`apr_clips = 48`, `may_clips = 48 / 2 = 24`, `(n+3)^2 = 196`).

## 4. Ablation C: Continuous Latent Recurrence &amp; `RingBufferLatentTee`

While Telegraphic CoT eliminates syntactic tokens, it still forces every remaining state transition through discrete vocabulary projection $W_{\text{unembed}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times \lvert V \rvert}$ and restricts intermediate representations to single-token embeddings rather than dense superpositions in $\mathbb{R}^d$.

To remove the discrete tokenization bottleneck entirely during intermediate reasoning, **Continuous Latent Looping** ([`experiments/src/latent_loop.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/latent_loop.py)) initializes $z_0 = h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ at the end of the prompt and applies a lightweight gated residual adapter $f_{\theta}: \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^d$ for $k = 1, \dots, K$ recurrent steps before invoking $W_{\text{unembed}}$ strictly once to emit the final answer:<Sidenote id={4} title="Adapter Parameterization">In [`latent_loop.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/latent_loop.py), $f_{\theta}$ is parameterized as a SwiGLU residual block $f_{\theta}(z) = (\operatorname{SiLU}(z W_{\text{gate}}) \odot (z W_{\text{up}})) W_{\text{down}},$ adding under ${1.8\%}$ parameter overhead while bypassing $\mathbb{R}^{d \times \lvert V \rvert}$ unembedding across steps ${1 \dots K.}$</Sidenote>

$$
z_0 = h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d, \qquad z_k = \operatorname{RMSNorm}\!\left(z_{k-1} + f_{\theta}(z_{k-1})\right) \in \mathbb{R}^d \quad \text{for } k \in \{1, \dots, K\}
$$

### Resolving the Interpretability-Efficiency Dilemma via `RingBufferLatentTee`

A pervasive objection to continuous latent reasoning is **opacity**: if the model reasons in $\mathbb{R}^d$ without emitting text tokens, how can practitioners audit intermediate steps or diagnose reasoning failures without incurring $O(K d)$ trajectory memory growth or gradient interference?

We resolve this tension with `RingBufferLatentTee` and `SidecarProbe` in [`experiments/src/latent_loop.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/latent_loop.py) and [`experiments/src/qwen3_ablation.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/qwen3_ablation.py) (continuously verified in [`experiments/tests/test_schema_export.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/tests/test_schema_export.py) and [`experiments/tests/test_qwen3_pytorch.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/tests/test_qwen3_pytorch.py)):

$$
\tilde{z}_k = \operatorname{sg}[z_k] \in \mathbb{R}^d, \qquad \ell_k = \operatorname{RMSNorm}(\tilde{z}_k) W_{\text{unembed}} \in \mathbb{R}^{\lvert V \rvert}, \qquad \nabla_{\theta_{\text{backbone}}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}}\!\left(\phi; \operatorname{sg}[z_{1:K}], y\right) = 0
$$

1. **Stop-Gradient Tap &amp; Disjoint Probe Optimization ($\nabla_{\theta_{\text{backbone}}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}} = 0$)**: `RingBufferLatentTee.push(z_k)` unconditionally invokes `z_k.detach()` ($\tilde{z}_k = \operatorname{sg}[z_k]$) *before* storing states in the buffer or passing them to `SidecarProbe` ($g_{\phi}$) or `LogitLens`. Furthermore, `SidecarProbe.step_probe_optimizer(...)` optimizes $\mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}}$ in a separate optimizer step strictly over probe weights $\phi$, ensuring auxiliary readouts never backpropagate into $\theta_{\text{backbone}}$ or force the backbone to encode surface grammar.
2. **Fixed-Capacity $O(1)$ Circular Buffer ($M \le K$) &amp; Zero-Overhead Production Default**: `RingBufferLatentTee` allocates a fixed circular buffer of capacity $M \le K$, bounding activation memory overhead to $O(M d) = O(1)$ with respect to total recurrence depth $K$. By default in production (`enable_latent_tee=False` in [`experiments/src/config.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/config.py)), `push(z_k)` is an immediate $O(1)$ no-op; setting `enable_latent_tee=True` enables on-demand Logit Lens and `SidecarProbe` decoding of the latest $M$ transitions.

## 5. Related Work &amp; Architectural Positioning

While our core latent recurrence builds on recent advances in continuous and recurrent-depth transformers, GITLTYN enforces three explicit architectural differentiations in [`experiments/src/latent_loop.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/latent_loop.py) and [`experiments/src/config.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/config.py):

### 1. Continuous Thought Without Gradient Interference vs. Coconut (Hao et al., 2024)
**Coconut** (*Chain of Continuous Thought*; Hao et al., 2024) feeds the terminal hidden state $h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ directly back as the next input embedding, bypassing $W_{\text{unembed}}$, but creates an unauditable black-box trajectory in $\mathbb{R}^d$ and suffers accuracy degradation ($\sim 8\%$ on GSM8K) without multi-stage curriculum training. Conversely, attaching naive auxiliary readout heads directly to $z_k$ contaminates latent representations with surface syntax gradients. By enforcing `.detach()` ($\operatorname{sg}[z_{1:K}]$) at the `RingBufferLatentTee` tap point prior to `SidecarProbe` ($g_{\phi}$) or `LogitLens` projection—and optimizing $\mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}}$ via a disjoint optimizer step ($\nabla_{\theta_{\text{backbone}}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}} = 0$)—our detachable tee + stop-gradient sidecar resolves Coconut's interpretability black-box problem with zero gradient interference on the primary $\mathbb{R}^d$ transition dynamics.

### 2. Bounded $O(1)$ Trajectory Memory vs. Huginn / Recurrent Depth (Geiping et al., 2025) &amp; Looped Transformers (Giannou et al., 2023)
**Looped Transformers** (Giannou et al., 2023) and recurrent-depth models such as **Huginn** (Geiping et al., 2025) scale test-time compute by looping transformer blocks $K$ times in latent space before token emission, which leads to unbounded $O(K d)$ activation memory growth if intermediate trajectories are retained for inspection. Unlike standard recurrent depth unrolling, our `RingBufferLatentTee` is implemented as a fixed-capacity circular buffer of size $M \le K$ ($O(1)$ memory overhead with respect to total recurrence depth $K$ during inference) paired with `enable_latent_tee=False` by default in production (`True` for mechanistic auditing)—strictly bounding activation memory while preserving on-demand mechanistic readouts of the latest $M$ recurrent transitions.

### 3. Motivational Syntactic Control vs. Compressed CoT (TokenSkip, CCoT, Sketch-of-Thought)
Compressed-CoT pipelines—including **CCoT** (Cheng &amp; Van Durme, 2024), **TokenSkip** (Xia et al., 2025), and **Sketch-of-Thought** (Aytes et al., 2025)—treat token pruning or abbreviated text sketching as the final inference efficiency method, continuing to pay $W_{\text{unembed}}$ projection FLOPs and KV-cache growth for every retained token. In GITLTYN, `telegraphic_cot` ([`experiments/src/prune_syntax.py`](https://github.com/ConstCorrectness/GITLTYN/blob/main/experiments/src/prune_syntax.py)) is kept strictly as a **motivational ablation baseline** alongside `verbose_cot` and budget-forced verbose CoT (`compute_matched` / `budget_forced_cot`). Telegraphic CoT is not our final proposed architecture; it serves as an empirical control proving that English grammatical scaffolding accounts for $64.6\%$–$67.1\%$ of emitted tokens with $< 1\%$ accuracy contribution, motivating the shift to pure $\mathbb{R}^d$ Continuous Latent Looping.
