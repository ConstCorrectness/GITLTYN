\section{Related Work and Architectural Positioning}
\label{sec:related-work}

Continuous Latent Looping with a Detachable Ring-Buffer Latent Tee intersects three active threads in test-time reasoning efficiency: continuous thought recurrence, recurrent-depth transformers, and discrete Chain-of-Thought (CoT) compression. While our core recurrence $z_k = \operatorname{RMSNorm}(z_{k-1} + f_{\theta}(z_{k-1})) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ shares latent-space state evolution with recent architectures, our systems framing and auditability invariants establish three explicit differentiations.

\paragraph{Continuous Thought Without Gradient Interference vs.\ Coconut.}
Coconut~\cite{hao2024coconut} feeds the terminal top-layer hidden state $h_t^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ directly back as the next input embedding, bypassing the vocabulary unembedding projection $W_{\text{unembed}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times \lvert V \rvert}$. However, operating purely in $\mathbb{R}^d$ yields an unauditable black-box trajectory and incurs notable accuracy degradation ($\approx 8\%$ on GSM8K) in the absence of multi-stage curriculum supervision. Conversely, attaching naive auxiliary decoding heads directly to $z_k$ risks forcing the backbone to allocate representational capacity to surface English syntax. In GITLTYN, auxiliary readout heads never backpropagate into the continuous thought loop: \texttt{RingBufferLatentTee.push($z_k$)} enforces an explicit stop-gradient boundary $\tilde{z}_k = \operatorname{sg}[z_k]$ (\texttt{z\_k.detach()}) prior to passing buffered states to either the \texttt{SidecarProbe} $g_{\phi}(\operatorname{sg}[z_{1:K}])$ or \texttt{LogitLens} projection, and the probe objective $\mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}}(\phi; \operatorname{sg}[z_{1:K}], y)$ is optimized via a disjoint optimizer step such that
\begin{equation}
  \nabla_{\theta_{\text{backbone}}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{probe}} = 0.
\end{equation}
Our detachable tee and stop-gradient sidecar thus resolve Coconut's interpretability black-box problem with zero gradient interference on primary $\mathbb{R}^d$ transition dynamics.

\paragraph{Bounded $O(1)$ Trajectory Memory vs.\ Recurrent Depth and Looped Transformers.}
Looped Transformers~\cite{giannou2023looped} and recurrent-depth models such as Huginn~\cite{geiping2025huginn} scale test-time compute by iterating transformer blocks $K$ times in latent space prior to token emission. Retaining full intermediate hidden trajectories across $K$ steps for mechanistic diagnosis causes activation memory to grow linearly as $O(K d)$. We implement \texttt{RingBufferLatentTee} as a fixed-capacity circular buffer of size $M \le K$, guaranteeing $O(M d) = O(1)$ activation memory overhead with respect to total recurrence depth $K$ during inference. Furthermore, the tee is governed by a runtime boolean flag (\texttt{enable\_latent\_tee=False} by default in production; \texttt{True} during mechanistic auditing) so inspection detaches cleanly with zero compute or memory overhead when not required. Unlike standard recurrent depth unrolling, our Ring-Buffer Latent Tee strictly bounds activation memory while preserving on-demand mechanistic readouts of the latest $M$ recurrent transitions.

\paragraph{Motivational Syntactic Control vs.\ Discrete CoT Compression.}
Recent CoT compression methods—including CCoT~\cite{cheng2024ccot}, TokenSkip~\cite{xia2025tokenskip}, and Sketch-of-Thought~\cite{aytes2025sketch}—treat pruned or abbreviated token sequences as the target inference architecture, continuing to incur per-token $W_{\text{unembed}}$ projection FLOPs and KV-cache expansion on all surviving tokens. Within GITLTYN, \texttt{telegraphic\_cot} is strictly retained as a \emph{motivational ablation baseline} alongside unconstrained \texttt{verbose\_cot} and budget-forced CoT (\texttt{compute\_matched} / \texttt{budget\_forced\_cot}, which truncates verbose CoT to the exact telegraphic token budget). Telegraphic CoT is not our proposed final architecture; rather, it serves as an empirical control proving that English grammatical scaffolding accounts for $64.6\%$--$67.1\%$ of emitted CoT tokens while contributing $< 1\%$ to task accuracy, directly motivating the shift to pure $\mathbb{R}^d$ Continuous Latent Looping.
